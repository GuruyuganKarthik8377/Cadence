Cadence — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Cadence is a habit-tracking app. This policy describes exactly what data the app stores and how it is used. Cadence has no ads, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and never sells data.

Data we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (handled by Supabase Auth; the password is stored only as a secure hash).
  • Profile: username, display name, an optional avatar image, and an optional short bio.
  • Achievements: which milestones you have unlocked and the date each one unlocked.
  • App content: the habits you create (names, schedules, reminder times, optional cover images), your daily completion history including any amount and free-text note you attach to a completed day, and your friendships/habit links with other users.
  • Location (optional, per habit): see Location below. Only collected if you attach a place to a habit.
  • Movement (optional, per habit): for habits that involve running or walking, the app reads your device's motion sensors while a run is in progress to count steps and verify the activity. Step counts are stored with the session; no route or map data is recorded.
  • Device token: if you enable notifications, a push notification token for your device.

Location

Location is entirely optional and off unless you choose it. If you attach a place to a habit (for example, your gym), the app stores the coordinates of that place and, when you tap “Check In”, reads your device's current location at that moment to confirm you are within range.

  • Location is read only while the app is open and only when you tap Check In. Cadence does not request or use background location, and does not track your movements.
  • Your live position is used for the distance comparison and is not stored or transmitted anywhere — only the pass/fail result becomes a completion.
  • The saved place for a habit is stored with that habit and is visible to you only.
  • Place search is powered by OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service. When you type a search, that text is sent to OpenStreetMap to return matching places.
  • You can remove a habit's location at any time by editing the habit.

Where it is stored

All data is stored in a Supabase project hosted in the ap-south-1 (Mumbai) region. Access is protected by row-level security: only you (and, for social features, your accepted friends) can read your data.

Stored on your device

So the app works without a network connection, Cadence keeps a copy of your habit list and recent completions in the app's private storage on your device, along with any check-offs made offline that are waiting to be uploaded. This copy is deleted when you sign out. If you add the home screen widget, the names of the habits due today are also stored on the device so the widget can display them.

What other users can see

Your profile (username, display name, avatar) is searchable by other users so friends can find you, along with your bio if you write one. A few account settings live on the same record and are readable by other signed-in users: your time zone (stored so reminders and daily rollovers land at the right local time) and your notification and streak-freeze preferences. Users you accept as friends can see your habit names, your unlocked achievements, and, for habits you explicitly link together, your completion activity. Friends can also react to your completions and send you a nudge about a linked habit; those reactions and nudges record who sent them and when. Nobody outside your accepted friends can see your data. Habit notes, amounts, and locations are never shared.

Notifications

Habit reminders are scheduled on your device and do not involve any server — the reminder time never leaves your phone for this purpose. If you enable notifications, a push token is stored so friends' nudges can reach you when the app is closed; it is used for nothing else. You can turn notifications off in the app, or in your device settings, at any time.

Data deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Privacy → Delete Account inside the app. Deletion is immediate and irreversible. See also account deletion instructions.
