Delete Your Cadence Account

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Cadence and go to Profile → Privacy.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

This immediately and permanently erases your account and all associated data: profile, habits (including any saved locations), reminder settings, completion history with its amounts and notes, streak freezes, run sessions, friendships, habit links, reactions and nudges you sent, notification tokens, and uploaded images. It cannot be undone.

Signing out also clears the copy of your habits held on the device for offline use, and removes anything shown by the home screen widget.

By email

If you can no longer sign in, email harishram.b06@gmail.com from the address you registered with, and your account and data will be deleted within 30 days.

What is deleted

Everything. Cadence keeps no residual copies of your content after deletion; only transient backups expire automatically on the infrastructure provider's standard schedule.
